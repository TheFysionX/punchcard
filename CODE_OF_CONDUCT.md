# Code of conduct

Punchcard contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful, and productive.

Be direct about technical disagreement without attacking people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use sexualized or degrading language. Respect requests to stop an unwanted interaction.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct harms the project or its community. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and a consistent path to correction.

Report conduct issues privately through the repository's security contact when public discussion would expose or escalate harm. Good-faith reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
